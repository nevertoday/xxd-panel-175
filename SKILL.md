## Panel-specific overlay

Use a miniature layered paper-cut bas-relief occupying only 20–25% of the design region, leaving 75–80% clean whitespace. Retain fibres, cut edges, slight impressions and thin paper layers. Use a warm ivory tonal palette with soft amber backlighting and light tea/sand/ochre shadows, not grey-black. Red or deep-ink accents must occupy less than 1%. Avoid a huge subject, full landscape, vivid yellow, plastic 3D and elaborate detail.
